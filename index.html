The UK
Hotspot Register

A working catalogue of the UK's high-strangeness clusters: places where sightings, cryptids, lights and the unexplained pile up in one patch of ground. Each entry is rated on two axes and sorted by how well it actually holds up.

How each site is scored

A · StrangenessHow anomalous and how varied the reported phenomena are. High means many kinds of strangeness, tightly clustered.
B · EvidenceQuality of the record: witness calibre, contemporaneous documentation, physical traces, independence from tabloid amplification.
ThresholdThe dashed marker sits at 60, the working bar for a case worth serious follow-up.

Method

Scores are an editorial starting point, not a verdict. Strangeness and evidence are judged separately on purpose: a site can be wildly strange and poorly evidenced, or mundane and well recorded. Tune the numbers as you dig. The aim is to keep the well-attested cases and the tabloid-amplified ones visibly apart.

Adding a report

Open assets/js/data.js, find the SITES array, and copy one block. Fill in the fields, save, commit. Filters and totals rebuild themselves from the data.
