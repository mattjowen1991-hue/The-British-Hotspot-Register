# Register: Prosaic Explanations Pass

For each hotspot, the mundane candidates spelled out: each explanation, a one-line "why
this is plausible," and a vetted source. This is the detailed companion to the one-line
"skeptical read" already on each card. Making the case for the ordinary explanation as
carefully as the strange one is the point of the project.

Source strength tiers as before: `primary`, `reputable`, `weak`.

No em dashes. Plain hyphens only.

## UK-001 Rendlesham Forest

- **Orford Ness lighthouse.** Its beam, five miles off, flashes through the trees on the exact bearing the airmen faced, matching the "pulsing" light they chased. [Ian Ridpath, reputable]
- **A bright fireball.** An exceptionally bright meteor burned up over southern England at almost the moment of the first 3am sighting, explaining the "object descending into the forest." [Ian Ridpath, reputable]
- **The ground marks were natural.** A local forester, Vince Thurkettle, identified the "landing indentations" as rabbit diggings and the broken branches as ordinary forest damage. [Ian Ridpath, reputable]
- **Radiation readings were normal.** Halt's own Geiger-counter figures sat within natural background levels, not the elevated readings later claimed. [Ian Ridpath, reputable]

Source: http://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham.html

## UK-002 Cannock Chase

- **The 2015 sighting was likely aircraft or a drone.** Witnesses heard a loud drone before seeing a slow, low object; the British UFO Research Association did not rule out a large drone or military prototype. [Sky HISTORY, reputable; Birmingham Mail via archive, reputable]
- **The black-eyed-child "wave" tracked the tabloids.** The 2014 surge followed three Daily Star front pages tied to the sale of a "haunted" pub, not a rise in independent reports. [Sky HISTORY, reputable]
- **Big-cat and dogman reports rarely survive scrutiny.** UK "black panther" and hairy-creature sightings recur nationwide and almost never yield a body, a clear photo, or physical evidence. [Sky HISTORY, reputable]

Sources: https://www.history.co.uk/shows/alien-files-unsealed/articles/top-ufo-spots-in-the-uk ; https://www.theufochronicles.com/2015/03/ufo-sighting-in-cannock-chase.html ; https://www.expressandstar.com/news/local-hubs/staffordshire/stafford/2020/06/02/staffordshire-town-named-as-ufo-hotspot/

## UK-003 Bonnybridge

- **Aircraft and flight paths.** The area sits near busy corridors between Glasgow and Edinburgh; many "objects" fit planes, helicopters and their lights. [BBC News, reputable]
- **A reputation that fed itself.** Once Bonnybridge was famous, reporting rose and the story attracted publicity, some of it stunt-driven; the University of Glasgow project frames the flap as substantially media-shaped. [UFOs.ac.uk, reputable]
- **Ordinary astronomical and atmospheric objects.** With hundreds of casual public reports a year, Venus, satellites and lanterns account for a large share. [BBC News, reputable]

Sources: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cjwngn293pvo ; https://ufos.ac.uk/bonnybridge/

## UK-004 Todmorden

- **Adamski died of a heart attack.** That was the coroner's medical finding; the open verdict reflects unexplained circumstances, not a supernatural cause. [Yorkshire Live, reputable]
- **The "materialised" body may be simple inattention.** The foreman's claim that it appeared from nowhere rests on his not having noticed it on an earlier pass. [Yorkshire Live, reputable]
- **Godfrey's abduction memory came from hypnosis.** The detailed encounter was recovered under regression, a method prone to confabulation and not reliable as a record of events. [Sky HISTORY, reputable]

Sources: https://www.examinerlive.co.uk/news/west-yorkshire-news/unsolved-baffling-case-missing-miner-14150127 ; https://www.history.co.uk/articles/the-todmorden-ufo-mystery-a-close-encounter-in-west-yorkshire

## UK-005 Warminster

- **Military airspace explains the sounds and lights.** A garrison town ringed by Salisbury Plain's ranges produces aircraft noise, sonic booms and live-firing, the raw material of "the Thing." [Wikipedia: Arthur Shuttlewood, reputable]
- **The emblematic photo was a hoax.** The 1965 Gordon Faulkner image, the case's most famous "evidence," was later exposed as fake. [Wikipedia: Arthur Shuttlewood, reputable]
- **The flap was journalist-driven.** Shuttlewood created and sustained the story, and organised skywatches generated their own sightings through expectation; he later claimed telepathic alien contact. [Warminster Journal, reputable]

Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Shuttlewood ; https://warminsterjournal.co.uk/ufo-expert-gives-verdict-on-warminster-thing-60-years-on/

## UK-006 Broad Haven

- **Mass suggestion among children.** The MoD's own investigators privately suspected pranksters and mass hysteria; a schoolyard rumour can spread and shape what pupils believe they saw. [The National Archives, primary]
- **Military overflights.** The RAF officer sent to investigate noted the area was crossed by many civilian and military aircraft, near RAF Brawdy. [BBC News, reputable]
- **A prankster in silver.** Contemporary speculation, and one later claim about a base fireman in a silver fire-suit, offers a mundane candidate for the "silver figures." (Weak, unverified.) [Herald.Wales, reputable]

Sources: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk ; https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/av/uk-wales-38860167 ; https://herald.wales/west-wales/pembrokeshire/the-welsh-triangle-what-was-seen-in-the-sky-back-in-1977/

## UK-007 Longdendale Valley

- **Hikers' torches.** Strings of moving lights match walkers on the tops; the local Mountain Rescue team logged the reports and repeatedly found no one there. [Dr David Clarke, reputable]
- **Distant car headlights.** On the hidden bends of the Woodhead Pass, headlights appear and vanish in ways that read as "dancing lights" from a distance. [Dr David Clarke, reputable]
- **Earth lights.** Luminous effects linked to tectonic stress in the rock, studied in the Pennines under "Project Pennine," fit some of the stranger reports. [Wikipedia: Longdendale lights, reputable]

Sources: https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/spooklights/longdendale-lights/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longdendale_lights

## UK-008 Bempton

- **A single collector shapes the record.** With almost all reports gathered and framed by one researcher, selection and interpretation are doing work no independent body checks. [The Scarborough News, reputable]
- **Coastal lights have many mundane sources.** Ships, offshore lights, aircraft from former airfields and the general sea-fret of a clifftop coast supply plenty of ambiguous lights. [The Scarborough News, reputable]
- **Folklore accretion.** An old coast with layered legend invites new experiences to be fitted into an existing "strange place" frame. (Sourcing here is thin; noted honestly.)

Source: https://www.thescarboroughnews.co.uk/news/author-back-with-more-strange-tales-713207

## UK-009 Berwyn Mountains

- **A recorded earthquake.** The British Geological Survey logged a magnitude 3.5 quake at 20:38 that night, epicentre near Bala, explaining the "explosion," the shaking and the sudden fear. [British Geological Survey, primary]
- **A bright meteor.** A bolide widely seen over Wales and northern England accounts for the lights streaking across the sky. [Wikipedia: Berwyn, reputable]
- **Earthquake lights.** Luminous ground-level effects associated with seismic stress fit the glows witnesses saw on the hills. [Andy Roberts, reputable]
- **The "cover-up team" were scientists.** Six investigators doing door-to-door enquiries were BGS staff, not a body-recovery unit; the radiation and leukaemia-cluster claims were later shown to be unfounded. [Andy Roberts, reputable]

Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berwyn_Mountain_UFO_incident ; http://www.uk-ufo.org/condign/berwart.htm

## UK-010 Pentyrch

- **Real military activity.** Low-flying aircraft including Chinooks were genuinely over Pentyrch that night, which accounts for the helicopters, the noise and the road closures hundreds of residents reported. [Metabunk, reputable]
- **Misidentified operations under stress.** In darkness and alarm, flares, drones and training kit can read as extraordinary craft. [Metabunk, reputable]
- **The spectacle is single-sourced.** The pyramid craft and ejected orbs trace largely to one witness's account; stress and expectation shape perception. [The Cardiffian, reputable]
- *Caveat: the tidy "Exercise Chameleon" label is itself disputed (that exercise appears to have run in northern England the week before), so the honest prosaic case is "military activity of some kind," not that specific exercise.*

Sources: https://www.metabunk.org/threads/the-pentyrch-ufo-encounter.11738/ ; https://cardiffjournalism.co.uk/thecardiffian/2022/01/21/military-are-hiding-the-facts-about-pentyrch-ufo-sighting-says-author/
